About the Studio

Design and engineering, in one pair of hands.

Zayya exists to close the gap between how a product looks and how it actually works. Every interface is designed with motion, accessibility and performance baked in from day one.

  • Design systems that scale Reusable components, tokens and patterns — not one-off screens.
  • Performance as a feature Fast loads, smooth animations, and no layout jank on mobile.
  • Accessible by default Keyboard support, reduced-motion handling and real contrast ratios.
